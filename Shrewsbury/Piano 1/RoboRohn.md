**RoboRohn** is a fifth-hand cyberware shop buried deep in the ruins of the lower city. The walls drip with condensation and rust, lit by the dying pulse of a broken neon sign. Inside, used implants twitch on stained trays, and the smell of solder, blood, and burned plastic fills the air.

The shop is run by **Kael "Rohn" Vorn**, a disgraced street surgeon known for his cold precision and total lack of empathy. His mechanical hands move without hesitation, whether he's installing a hacked optic or gutting a corpse for salvage.

With him works **Aika-7**, a repurposed android assistant with fading synthetic skin and a silent, watchful presence. She speaks rarely, moves like a machine built for something gentler, and handles both tools and threats with the same calm efficiency.

RoboRohn serves clients with low humanity and deep ties to illegal circuits—gangs, smugglers, and those too broke or too wanted to go anywhere else. No questions, no records, no refunds.
