From the Latin _arena_, this ancient ruin has been gutted and reassembled into a brutal underground fight pit. Known simply as **Arenas**, it’s a bloodstained monument to violence, where combat is raw, illegal, and often lethal. Killing isn't just allowed—it’s sometimes expected.

Matches are held nightly, some for money, others for revenge. The most infamous are fought in **"the Swamp"**, a lower pit filled with decades of congealed blood, oil, and sludge. Movement is slow, footing is treacherous, and everything stinks of rot and metal. The crowd loves it.

Hidden behind the bleachers and rusted catwalks, two **Italian mobsters from Little Italy** run an illicit gambling ring. Their operation is small but profitable, and deeply tied to the local crime web. **Selling them out to their boss**—a cold, untouchable don—might earn the players a valuable favor, the kind only blood can buy.
### **1. Salvatore "Sal" Bracco**

- **Age**: 49
    
- **Physical Description**: Stocky build, short and broad-shouldered. Greasy black hair slicked back, thick gold rings on every finger. His face is scarred from an old blade fight, and his right eye is milky white—lost in a bet he refused to pay.
    
- **Personality**: Loud, vulgar, and always chewing something. He’s the talker of the duo, charming when he wants to be, terrifying when he doesn’t. Likes to make jokes before pulling triggers.

### **2. Enzo "Knives" Bellini**

- **Age**: 42
    
- **Physical Description**: Tall, wiry, and always dressed in a blood-red suit with a black shirt underneath. His face is clean-shaven, expressionless, and his skin is covered in faded prison tattoos. Always wears thin gloves and carries at least four knives on him.
    
- **Personality**: Quiet, observant, and intensely paranoid. Enzo rarely speaks, but when he does, it's to end arguments. He's fast, efficient, and rumored to have killed in the ring before becoming a bookie.
