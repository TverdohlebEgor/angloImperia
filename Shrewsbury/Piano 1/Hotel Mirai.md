Once a luxury tower suite, now a shattered husk—**Hotel Mirai** hangs like a broken tooth from the second level of the upper city. A massive hole in the ceiling gapes open to the skies above, letting cold winds and acid rain pour into the upper halls. The structure **fell from the tier above**, during one of the great collapses, and was “rebuilt” hastily—if at all.

Today, it’s a ruin.

The marble floors are cracked, mold creeps along every wall, and the power flickers unreliably. Most locals avoid it. It’s home to squatters, addicts, and the desperate, who shuffle through its halls in silence. The staircases are unstable. The elevators don’t work. The second floor is open to the air—birds, drones, and death.

But deep within the wreckage, in rooms blocked by debris and false walls, hides a clandestine cell of the [[NGH]]
They operate silently, **abducting lone drifters** or vulnerable nomads to harvest implants, test neural drugs, or worse. Most never see them. The unlucky hear whispers in the dark before vanishing forever.

Players might come looking for salvage, a lost contact, or shelter. But at **Hotel Mirai**, the deeper you go, the less chance you come back.
