**UMB** is a decaying chemical farm on the edge of the city's industrial dead zone. The sky above it is permanently blackened by thick tar-like smoke belching from corroded stacks. Toxic runoff seeps into cracked concrete, and the air tastes of rust, acid, and burnt lungs.

Dozens of low-paid operators work inside—most infected, coughing up blood, their skin pocked with chemical burns. They’re trapped by debt, addiction, or threats. Few live past 35.

Hidden among the sprawl and toxicity, a secretive cell of **Purists**—anti-industrial radicals—has infiltrated the outer perimeter. They're planning to **shut UMB down permanently**, using sabotage and viral explosives. Gaining their trust is hard; they assume everyone’s corrupted or compromised. But if players manage to contact them and assist their mission, they’ll be **heavily rewarded**, both in credits and rare resources.

---

### **The Four Purists**

#### 1. **Lira Voss**

- **Age**: 31
    
- **Physical Description**: Thin, wiry frame with deep-set grey eyes and shaved black hair. Wears a rebreather mask almost constantly. Skin is pale from living underground, and her forearms are covered in scarred anarchist symbols carved by hand.
    
- **Role**: Leader and tactician. Cold, calculating, and extremely paranoid.
    

#### 2. **Denton Krail**

- **Age**: 44
    
- **Physical Description**: Heavyset with a broken nose, thick cybernetic jaw, and stained yellow eyes from chemical exposure. Bald, with a robotic left arm repurposed from mining equipment.
    
- **Role**: Demolition expert. Quiet but deadly; prefers actions over talk.
    

#### 3. **Mireya "Echo" Sol**

- **Age**: 26
    
- **Physical Description**: Slim, athletic, with long braided auburn hair and one glowing green cyber-eye. Usually wears patched scavenger armor over loose robes. Voice distorted by a throat modulator damaged in an earlier UMB raid.
    
- **Role**: Infiltration and sabotage. Bold, fast, and often reckless.
    

#### 4. **Gregor Lint**

- **Age**: 52
    
- **Physical Description**: Gaunt, with a heavily wrinkled face and sunken cheeks. Long silver beard tied in knots, eyes always hidden behind black industrial goggles. Moves with a limp from an old injury.
    
- **Role**: Chemist and virus engineer. Former UMB scientist turned whistleblower. Calm, poetic, and quietly vengeful.
