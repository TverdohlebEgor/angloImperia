Hidden in a crumbling overpass and lit by swaying wire-lights, **The Street Market** is where the city breathes. Here, vendors and smugglers trade everything from stolen biochips to synthetic spices. Languages mix in a haze of steam, neon, and tension. Everyone lies, and everyone’s armed.

The air stinks of cheap fry oil, engine grease, and pheromone perfume. Drones hover overhead for surveillance—or assassination. The stalls shift daily, and alliances are made and broken faster than credit can change hands.

For players, The Street Market is full of **opportunity**—and **risk**. Valuable contacts, rare gear, black-market cyberware, or just a way out. But step wrong, and someone might follow you home with a knife in their coat.

---

### **5 Key Figures at The Street Market**

#### 1. **Jinx**

- **Role**: Tech scavenger and cyberware dealer
    
- **Appearance**: Short, hyperactive, with patchy blue hair and goggles welded into her forehead.
    
- **Opportunities**: Sells illegal implants and rare pre-Collapse tech. Can upgrade gear in exchange for favors.
    
- **Dangers**: She rigs everything she sells with kill-switches.
    

---

#### 2. **Father Uro**

- **Role**: Street preacher turned organ broker
    
- **Appearance**: Towering, bald, dressed in stained priest robes with cybernetic arms.
    
- **Opportunities**: Pays handsomely for rare organic components or fresh cyber-organs.
    
- **Dangers**: Has muscle everywhere and a twisted morality—he might drug you and take what he needs.
    

---

#### 3. **Marta Two-Skin**

- **Role**: Fence and information broker
    
- **Appearance**: Middle-aged, gaunt, with her face half-covered by a second layer of living synthetic skin.
    
- **Opportunities**: Knows who's buying, who's hiding, and what’s about to explode.
    
- **Dangers**: Sells information to multiple sides. She’ll help you—then sell you out.
    

---

#### 4. **Kelso "Wirebite" Dent**

- **Role**: Freelance black ICE hacker
    
- **Appearance**: Wears a hooded suit patched with wires and ports, with a mechanical jaw constantly twitching.
    
- **Opportunities**: Can hack corp systems, disable drones, or forge IDs—for a price.
    
- **Dangers**: Addicted to neurotox cyberdrugs, prone to violent crashes or betrayal mid-job.
    

---

#### 5. **The Twins (Rik & Rek)**

- **Role**: Enforcers for a local gang
    
- **Appearance**: Identical synth-modded brothers, one painted red, the other blue. Speak in sync.
    
- **Opportunities**: Offer protection for bribes or info on rival crews. Sometimes run black-market fights.
    
- **Dangers**: Extremely violent. Kill for disrespect or late payments. Love chaos.
