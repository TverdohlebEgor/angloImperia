Tucked behind a collapsing holo-theater and half-hidden by scaffolding and static-filled signage, **CTS Paradise** is a dusty relic of another era. Inside, rows of flickering monitors, rusted terminals, tape drives, and forgotten keyboards form chaotic towers of obsolete tech. The place hums softly—not from electricity, but from age.

The shop specializes in **ancient digital hardware** and rare, pre-Singularity components. For most, it’s junk. For the right kind of operator, it’s treasure. Among the relics, there’s rumor of a **MetaInternet-to-Internet converter**—a fragile interface that could access buried data caches and ghost servers from before the Collapse. The kind of device that can rewrite destinies… if it works.

The **owner**, a jittery man named **Carlo Tesso**, is in his late 50s, bald with big lenses over tired eyes, always covered in dust and static burns. He’s protective of his wares and even more protective of himself.

He’s willing to part with the converter—but not cheap. Unless…

Carlo has fallen for a local woman from **Little Italy**, a kind-eyed Italian florist named **Renata**, but he’s too afraid to approach her. She’s under the “protection” of the same mafia that runs parts of Arenas and the black markets. He’s convinced that even speaking to her could get him killed.

If the players can help him **safely reach Renata**—or broker some kind of truce—they might walk away with the converter **free of charge**… or unlock access to even more forgotten tech Carlo keeps hidden in the back
